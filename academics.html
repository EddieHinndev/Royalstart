EDUCATION AT ROYALSTART

Preparing Learners for a Brighter Tomorrow

At RoyalStart Academy, we believe that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, grow and succeed. Our academic programme is designed to nurture curiosity, build knowledge and develop confidence.

We provide learning opportunities from early childhood through primary school, supporting learners at every stage of their educational journey.

Our goal is to develop well-rounded learners who are academically capable, creative, responsible and ready to face the opportunities of the future.

OUR LEARNING PROGRAMMES

Learning Levels

We welcome learners at different stages of development, providing a supportive foundation for lifelong learning.

Playgroup

A warm introduction to learning through play, exploration, interaction and early development.

PP1

Building early communication, social interaction, creativity and foundational learning skills.

PP2

Strengthening early literacy, numeracy, confidence and readiness for Grade 1.

Lower Primary

Grades 1–3. Developing essential literacy, numeracy, communication and problem-solving skills.

Upper Primary

Grades 4–6. Expanding knowledge, independent thinking, creativity and academic confidence.

Junior School

Grades 7–9. Encouraging deeper understanding, practical skills and preparation for future pathways.

EDUCATION FOR THE FUTURE

Digital Skills & Computer Education
